# Study sound credits

All three recordings are public domain (CC0 1.0) from [Freesound](https://freesound.org). No attribution is required; credit is given anyway.

| File | Original recording | Recorded by | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rain.mp3` | [Spring night rain](https://freesound.org/people/ali-k/sounds/156049/) | ali@k | CC0 |
| `fire.mp3` | [Fireplace](https://freesound.org/people/inchadney/sounds/132534/) | inchadney | CC0 |
| `cafe.mp3` | [Bar crowd in Belgrade](https://freesound.org/people/epicwizard/sounds/394290/) | epicwizard | CC0 |

How they were prepared: a steady section was cut from each recording (no thunder or sudden noises), crossfaded end to start for a seamless loop, matched to −23 LUFS, and encoded as 96 kbps MP3. Each file ends with 0.4 s of its own beginning so browsers can loop it without a gap.

The thunder and the computer hum are still generated live in the browser.
